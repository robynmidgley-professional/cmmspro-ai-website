Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to do formal CMMS training to use CMMSPro.AI?

No. You can safely use CMMSPro.AI Co-Pilot without formal CMMS training. Co-Pilot gives you access to the CMMS Masterclass, where you will learn how to make the CMMS casts and receive an introduction to the science of CMMS and clinical reasoning.

CMMS Catalyst provides you with full access to the CMMS Online Course — structured to help you learn key concepts and embed complex knowledge through animated and instructional videos, summary fact sheets, practical videos, journal articles and educational articles, with instant results on your answers.

What's included in CMMSPro.AI Catalyst?

Full access to the CMMS Online Course: 13 modules and 45 lessons. To complete the course and assess embedded knowledge and practical skill, you'll have the option to upload videos of your patient assessment, cast application, cast removal, and your patient's movement result.

You'll have 3 months to complete the course, with a progress dashboard to track how far you've come.

How are CMMSPro.AI and the CMMS course delivered?

CMMSPro.AI is delivered through a Membership Portal containing the CMMS Masterclass, training videos and publications library. The CMMSPro.AI chatbot lives inside the portal, accessible from any device — phone, tablet or PC.

As soon as you register, you'll receive your unique login and be taken into the members area with immediate access to the course materials. The platform remembers where you left off, so you can proceed at your own pace.

How long will it take to complete the CMMS online course?

For the best results, our students dedicate 2 weeks to the material so they can proceed without delay to the practical component of the course. The time to implement will vary based on your previous experience — and the good news is that you'll always have access to the material, to reference whenever and however often you need.

Is CMMSPro.AI right for me?

The people who get the biggest benefit from CMMSPro.AI are those who strive to improve their knowledge and skills through continued professional development — therapists who want to unlock the secret to resolving digital stiffness, improve patient outcomes, and be part of a global movement changing the way the stiff hand is treated.

There is not a single clinician — newly qualified or highly experienced — who will not benefit from CMMSPro.AI.
