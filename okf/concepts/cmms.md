# What it is

Casting Motion to Mobilise Stiffness (CMMS) is a hand therapy technique, developed from the foundational framework of Judy Colditz, that uses a purpose-designed cast to channel a patient's own active motion into stiff joints and restore movement. It is the clinical foundation CMMSPro.AI teaches and reasons from.

# The expertise behind it

CMMSPro.AI is based on Judy Colditz's framework and on Robyn Midgley's 25 years of clinical experience applying the technique, together with 6 years of mentoring therapists who completed her online CMMS training. The portal also gives therapists access to Robyn's publications.
