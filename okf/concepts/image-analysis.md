# What it is

Therapists upload images of the stiff hand — ideally an AP and a lateral view — for the mentor to analyse. Any image can be uploaded except X-rays or scans. The mentor interprets the degree of joint stiffness, the degree of swelling, and tissue status such as redness or wounds, and uses that reading to inform the mentoring discussion.

Images are uploaded anonymously and only with the patient's consent. See [data and privacy](/okf/concepts/data-privacy.md).
