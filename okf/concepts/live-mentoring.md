# What it is

Mentoring works in both text and voice. Therapists can use CMMSPro.AI on a phone, iPad, or laptop and discuss a case — with or without the patient present — and receive immediate help. "Real-time" means exactly that: guidance while the case is in front of them.

# Languages

The mentoring is fully conversational in ten languages: English, Dutch, Portuguese, French, Spanish, Arabic, Mandarin, Turkish, German, and Afrikaans.
