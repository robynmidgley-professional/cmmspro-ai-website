# What it is

Applying the CMMS technique well — whether you are newly qualified or experienced — takes skill, experience, and mentorship. CMMSPro.AI gives hand therapists an affordable, intelligent, and accessible AI mentor, available 24/7 in 10 languages, to support clinical reasoning, CMMS strategy, cast design, exercise prescription, and progress monitoring. The aim is safe and accurate application of the CMMS technique for the stiff hand.

It is the first AI mentor we are aware of built specifically for the stiff hand and the CMMS technique.

# The core promise

Immediate access to fast-tracked CMMS teaching and to a CMMS expert mentor who guides the therapist's clinical decision-making — in 10 languages, 24/7, at an affordable price, anywhere in the world.

# Who it is for

All hand therapists, at any level of experience. The therapist selects their level and the mentor responds accordingly: an entry-level therapist receives detailed step-by-step guidance, while an experienced therapist can have their clinical reasoning challenged at an advanced level. It is designed specifically for the care of patients with stiff hands and for therapists who want to learn and apply the CMMS technique.
