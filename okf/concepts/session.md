# What happens in a session

The therapist opens CMMSPro.AI on a private portal, selects their language and experience level, and enters their patient's information anonymously with a detailed clinical history. They upload two images for the AI mentor to analyse.

The session then moves through a five-stage pipeline: Intake, Analysis, Strategy, Implementation, and Results.

Throughout, a sidebar shows the CMMS cast images the mentor refers to, for easy visual reference. In the Strategy stage, relevant playbooks appear so the therapist can compare their case with a worked one. Teaching videos on how to make each cast are available in the portal, and the mentor can walk the therapist through the casting steps if they want that level of help.

The mentor discusses the case and questions the therapist — to surface red flags, offer a different perspective, and help decide on cast design, wearing schedule, exercises, the cast weaning period, cast changes, and casting tips or techniques to consider. It is thorough, and it holds the knowledge and the working detail so the therapist feels less overwhelmed.
