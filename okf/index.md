# CMMSPro.AI — Knowledge Bundle

CMMSPro.AI is an AI mentor for hand therapists learning and applying the CMMS technique (Casting Motion to Mobilise Stiffness) for the stiff hand. It provides affordable, 24/7 clinical-reasoning mentorship in 10 languages, alongside accredited CMMS training. Created by Robyn Midgley, a Specialist Hand Therapist with 25 years of experience.

Start a free 7-day trial at https://www.cmmspro.ai
