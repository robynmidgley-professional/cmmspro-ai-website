# Plans

CMMSPro.AI is offered on three tiers:

* Co-Pilot — $9.99/month. Access to the fast-track training and the AI mentor.
* Catalyst — $39.99/month. Adds full access to Robyn Midgley's accredited CMMS Online course and the CMMS Club.
* Legacy Member — $290/year, a founding-member rate limited to 25 therapists. Includes full accredited course access and the CMMS Club.

Catalyst and Legacy members receive full, accredited CMMS training — saving the cost and travel of an in-person course — plus monthly in-person mentoring through the CMMS Club on Zoom.

# Getting started

Therapists can start a free 7-day trial at https://www.cmmspro.ai

To talk through the tool and the CMMS technique first, therapists can book a CMMS discovery call at https://forms-robynmidgley.vercel.app/book
