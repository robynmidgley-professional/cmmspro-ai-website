# What it is

The portal includes around 5 hours of clinical lectures and videos showing how to make each cast design, designed to fast-track learning the CMMS technique.

CMMSPro.AI offers full, accredited CMMS training to Catalyst and Legacy members, who get full access to Robyn Midgley's CMMS Online course. This saves therapists the cost of travelling to an in-person course, paying upfront for the course, and related travel expenses. Co-Pilot members have access to the fast-track training and the AI mentor.
